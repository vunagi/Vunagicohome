Vunagi Black Foundation

Commerce with purpose.

A non-profit dedicated to expanding education access and building school infrastructure for children living in poverty across Southeast Asia — beginning in Thailand. Funded by every Vunagi&co purchase.

Japan and Thailand share a 50-year development partnership. The Foundation is built in the same spirit — a long-term investment in the generational change that only education can produce.
